About this site

Opti is an unofficial study companion for 15.C57 Optimization Methods at MIT. It is built from the lecture notes and the two course textbooks: Bertsimas and Tsitsiklis, Introduction to Linear Optimization (BT), and Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization (BV). The lecture notes define what is examinable. When something here disagrees with them, trust the notes.
